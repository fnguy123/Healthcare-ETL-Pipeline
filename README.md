### Key Data Categories Needed for UDS Reporting

* Patient Demographics (Table 3A/3B): Data on patient age, sex at birth, race, ethnicity, and insurance type (Medicare, Medicaid, private, uninsured).
* Patient Zip Codes (Table 4): Total patients by ZIP code of residence, which must match insurance totals.
* Clinical Outcomes & Measures (Tables 6A, 6B, 7): Data on services provided (e.g., visits, immunizations), clinical processes, and outcomes, including eCQMs (electronic Clinical Quality Measures).
* Staffing & Utilization (Table 5): Full-time equivalents (FTEs) by staff type, patient visits by discipline, and service usage.
* Financial Data (Tables 8A, 9E, 9D): Costs, revenues by source, and grant-related financial metrics.
